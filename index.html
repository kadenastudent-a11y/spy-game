MISSION BRIEFING & RULES

Read carefully before proceeding to your individual briefing.

Each team will send one member at a time to meet with the instructor in the hallway, the instructor will secretly tell that group if they are the spy or a regular person. Once all have been briefed, teams will break out into their four teams and ask each other background questions—spies should make up their entire backstory (except name, rank, etc.), while everyone else must tell the truth. Examples of personal things to make up, where they are from / hometown, hobbies, favorite sport teams, family. Afterward, all groups will reconvene and vote on who they think the spy was. This is based off of in WW2 when Gi’s would ask about things that only Americans would know. No protected categories. Their may be 0 or more than 1 spy per team.
